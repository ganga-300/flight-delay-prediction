\section{Feature Engineering}
\label{sec:features}

\subsection{Decision time and leakage policy}
Predictions are made at the end of day $D{-}1$ for every flight on day $D$, to support next-day
operational planning (crew, spare aircraft and gate buffers). A feature may use the published
schedule, including all of day $D$'s flights, and the outcomes of earlier flights known before
$D$; nothing realised on day $D$. Later cut-offs (e.g.\ two hours before departure) would need
same-day real-time data that this dataset cannot validate.

\subsection{Feature set}
The model uses 59 features (49 numeric, 10 categorical), fixed before any model was trained:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Calendar (10):} scheduled departure hour, day of week, weekend and U.S.\ federal
  holiday proximity, with sine/cosine encodings of hour and weekday.
  \item \textbf{Schedule and route (6):} scheduled departure and arrival minutes, scheduled block
  time, an overnight-arrival flag, distance and log-distance. Block time is computed on a common
  UTC basis using each airport's IANA time zone, because local clock differences mix duration with
  time-zone offsets (they imply negative taxi times for 39.2\% of flights).
  \item \textbf{Categorical (10):} carrier, origin, destination, route, departure-time bucket and
  five crosses (carrier with origin, destination, route and bucket; origin with bucket). Levels with
  fewer than 50 training rows are grouped as \emph{other}.
  \item \textbf{Congestion (4):} scheduled departures from the same origin in the same hour and day,
  for the same carrier and day, and the hour's share of the day, counted on the full published
  schedule.
  \item \textbf{Historical (29):} past-only delay rates for carrier, route, origin and destination
  over 1, 7 and 28 days and all prior days, with counts for the windowed rates, shrunk towards the
  global past rate with prior strength 20.
\end{itemize}
Month and day-of-year encodings are excluded: training covers January--August only, so every later
month would be outside the training range. Seasonal shifts are left to the past-only history
windows.

\subsection{Preventing temporal leakage}
A past flight contributes to history only from the New York date on which its outcome was known:
its actual arrival time, converted to New York time, plus a 30-minute reporting buffer. On the
training period this rule places no label before its arrival date, whereas a simpler
departure-plus-air-time estimate would have counted 19 labels a day early. The 30-minute buffer is
an assumption about outcome availability (a near-real-time status feed), not a measured value.
Learned transformations (rare-level grouping, median imputation, scaling and one-hot encoding)
are fitted on training data only. Automated tests check that rebuilding the features with all
later records removed leaves every earlier feature value unchanged, that no post-departure or
target-derived column enters the feature matrix, and that the feature set stays at the 59 frozen
features.
